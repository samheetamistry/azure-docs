> [!IMPORTANT]
> Azure Front Door (classic) retires on **March 31, 2027**. New profiles, new domain onboarding, and managed certificates are no longer supported. Complete your [migration to Azure Front Door Standard or Premium](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/frontdoor/migrate-tier) before retirement. After retirement, Microsoft plans to automatically migrate remaining eligible profiles on a best-effort basis; successful migration and uninterrupted service are not guaranteed. **Do not rely on automatic migration as your primary migration plan.** For more information, see the [Azure Front Door (classic) retirement FAQ](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/frontdoor/classic-retirement-faq).
